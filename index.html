Who you'd be working with

I started Furst Computer Services in 1980, and I've been helping people get their computers to do what they need ever since. AI is the newest tool in that same box. It's useful for some jobs and overhyped for others, and knowing which is which is most of what I bring.

Radio was my other career. I spent more than ten years in Oregon radio: program director at KWRO in Coquille, where I hosted Jim Furst in the Morning, then mornings at KYNG AM / K-105 FM in Coos Bay. I hold FCC First and Second Class licenses, and I wrote and produced commercials for local businesses along the way.

These days I live in Pāhoa, which puts me a short drive from most of Puna. When you call, you get me, not a call center.
